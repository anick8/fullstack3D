## Design Specificity Verdict
Film is authored and specific; the card layer is a category-interchangeable resume template (eyebrow + title + grey body ×6). Missing proof (Amul, Syngenta, links).
Detector: CLI 0 findings. Browser 9 elements / 12 flags: gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow ×7 (false positive — mandated Liquid Glass), hero-eyebrow-chip + kicker-above-heading ×5 (real, corroborates template sameness), dark-glow on Explore (minor).

## Priority Issues
1. [P0] Contact and proof unreachable until film ends — contact only at p 0–0.14 and ≥0.93; no Amul/Syngenta/LinkedIn/resume up front. Fix: persistent glass contact bar (email+copy, LinkedIn, GitHub, Resume), contact as primary CTA, proof line in intro, Flowshaala link. Commands: clarify, layout.
2. [P1] Cards overflow and trap scroll — Recent work 593/487px, Toolbox 573/487, Backend 490/487 at 698px viewport. Fix: split Now into two chapters, 2 bullets each; Skills to 3 groups; remove overflow-y-auto. Command: distill.
3. [P1] No-JS blank page and heavy preload — all cards SSR at opacity:0; 242 frames/17.9MB load at once incl. 13.2MB lr2 unused on touch. Fix: intro visible on SSR, skip lr2 on coarse pointers, defer transform frames, loading hint. Commands: harden, optimize.
4. [P2] Pacing — About (0.16–0.38, right) covers avatar close-up; 0.38–0.46 dead gap over cubicle reveal. Fix: shift About to ~0.20–0.40, caption card at ~0.42 carrying proof, or h-[700vh]. Command: layout.
5. [P2] Reduced motion still scrubs frames (HeroSequenceCanvas.jsx reduced-motion branch). Fix: static key frame per chapter with crossfade. Command: animate.
